2. Processing at a glance

This website may process the following categories of data:

Data processed

  • IP address of the requesting device,
  • date and time of access,
  • requested page or file and the referring page, if sent,
  • browser, operating system and device information, and
  • HTTP status, transferred data volume and error data.

Purpose and legal basis

Processing is necessary to deliver the website, maintain its stability and security, and investigate technical problems or attacks. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interests are the secure, reliable and efficient provision of this website.

Recipients and retention

The website is hosted by webgo GmbH, Wendenstraße 8–12, 20097 Hamburg, Germany. webgo receives the data required for delivery and operation of the website. Log data is erased or anonymised when it is no longer required for operation or security. It may be retained for longer where a specific security incident must be investigated or a legal obligation applies.

4. Contact by email and contact form

The contact form is implemented using a mailto: link. The website itself does not send your entries to a form service or store them on a web server. On submission, your device opens the configured email application. Your data is transmitted only when you send the prepared email there.

Data, purpose and legal basis

I process your name, email address, message, any attachments and technical email metadata to answer your inquiry and communicate with you. If your request concerns a contract or steps before entering into a contract, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. For other inquiries, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is appropriate and efficient communication.

Recipients and retention

Email data is processed by the email providers involved in its transmission and delivery. My mailbox is provided by 1&1 Mail & Media GmbH (GMX). I erase inquiries once they have been finally dealt with and no statutory retention duty or overriding interest in retaining evidence applies. In those cases, data is retained only for the relevant statutory or limitation period.

Provision of data

There is no legal or contractual obligation to contact me. The fields marked as required are needed to prepare the email and reply to it. The checkbox confirms that you have read this policy and agree to the processing described here. The applicable legal bases remain Article 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR as described above.

5. Language preference and local storage

If you actively choose “DE” or “EN”, the website stores the value in your browser's local storage under the key portfolio-language. On later visits, the value is read to display the language you selected. It is not sent to me or used for tracking.

Access to this information is necessary to provide the language setting expressly requested by you and is based on Section 25(2)(2) of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG). Insofar as the preference constitutes personal data, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR also applies; the legitimate interest is a user-friendly language setting.

The setting remains on your device until you overwrite it by choosing another language or delete the website data in your browser. This website does not use cookies or non-essential storage technologies. Accordingly, no consent banner is used for its current functionality.

6. External links

This website contains links to GitHub, LinkedIn and separate project websites. Merely displaying these links does not establish a connection to those providers. If you click a link, your browser accesses the destination and the relevant provider may process technical access data, including your IP address. The destination provider is responsible for that processing and its own privacy information applies.

Depending on the provider, data may also be processed outside the European Economic Area. Please consult the destination provider's privacy information before using its service.

7. Other recipients and international transfers

Personal data is disclosed only where necessary for the purposes described above, where a service provider is used, where I am legally required to do so, or where disclosure is necessary for legal claims. Possible recipient categories are hosting and email providers, IT service providers, courts and public authorities.

The hosting and email providers used by me and named above operate in Germany; no international transfer is intended for these processing activities. This does not include processing carried out independently by a destination provider after you click an external link. If a future service provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, a transfer will take place only under the requirements of Chapter V GDPR, such as an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards.
